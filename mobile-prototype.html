What is a Bull vs. Bear Market?
A bull market is when prices rise ≥ 20% from a recent low. A bear market is when prices fall ≥ 20% from a recent high. Knowing which one you're in changes how you size your positions.
Paper Trading Basics
Paper trading lets you practice with virtual cash and no real risk. Key concepts: average cost, unrealized P&L, and realized gain.
Reading a Forecast
Each ticker has a bear, base, and bull scenario. The confidence score tells you how reliable the base case is. Higher = more certainty, not a guarantee.
